Here is the steps of language pack installation.

 

・Copy the \"x64fre_Server_ja-jp_lp.cab\" to a folder in local drive on target computer.

              Path:      [\\\\10.74.202.248\\Share2\\Tools](file://10.74.202.248/Share2/Tools)

・Right click the START button and click \[运行[\].]

・In \"运行\" window, enter \"lpksetup\" and click \[确定[\].]

・In \"安装或卸载显示语言\" window, click \"安装显示语言\".

・In \"选择要安装的显示语言\" window, click \[浏览[\...\].]

・In \"浏览文件或文件夹\" window, select the \"x64fre_Server_ja-jp_lp.cab\" and click \[确定[\].]

・In \"选择要安装的显示语言\" window, check the following language pack and click \[下一步[\].]

              \-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\--

              语言                                  类型      大小      消息

              \-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\--

              \[✔[\] ]日语 (日本语)            MUI       248 MB

・In \"复查并接受 Microsoft 软件许可条款\" window, check \"我接受许可条款(A)。\" and click \[下一步[\].]

・In \"安装显示语言\" window, wait for the installation to complete.

・In \"安装显示语言\" window, confirm the progress is \"已完成\" then click \[关闭[\].]

・Right click the START button and select \"控制面板\".

・In \"调整计算机的设置\" window, click \"添加语言\".

・In \"更改语言首选项\" window, click \"添加语言\"..

・In \"添加语言\" window, select \"拼音R\" -\> \"日本语\" and click \[添加[\].]

・In \"更改语言首选项\" window, select the following language and click \"上移\" to move it on the top of the list.

              日本语                Windows 显示语言: 将在下次登录时启用

                                          输入法: 微软输入法

                                          日期､时间和数字格式

・Reboot OS manually.

・Sign in again and confirm the display language has been changed to Japanese.

 

Since the \"MapsBroker\" service problem is a different issue, please open a new case and create RA if customer still wants to solve it.
