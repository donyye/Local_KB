CPU报错的几种原因

关于服务器CPU CPU IERRs, machine check errors报错的，从之前cpu dispatch review 的结果看，

大家基本上除了让客户升级bios，修改bios 设置外（c state, c1e , max performance ）, 如果还有报错，基本也没做其他的检查就派CPU 了。

而事实上很多CPU 的报错并非CPU硬件问题引起。

从我们REVIEW 的结果看，也只有1/3的派单,dsp 有确认需要更换CPU 。

所以关于CPU 的派单，不要过于随意， 以下是我整理的一些其他原因引起CPU 的报错，希望对大家CPU 的troubleshooting 有所帮助。

另外涉及到CPU 的派单一定要严格执行POLICY！

一：KCS ID:583181 （不支持的操作系统和驱动引起的CPU 报错）

Problem: For 12G severs, a customer may report CPU IERRs, machine check errors, and performance issues that are NOT the result of faulty hardware. Data indicates that such errors are often caused by an unsupported operating system and/or the associated drivers. The unsupported operating systems include Ubuntu, CentOS, and Fedora.

对于12G的服务器，客户可能会报告说CPU IERRs, machine check errors，但又没有明显的硬件问题。诸如这样的错误很多事由于安装了不支持的操作系统和驱动引起的。不支持的操作系统包括Ubuntu, CentOS, and Fedora

Note: These same issues can be seen on supported  Linux operating systems under the right circumstances (e.g. drivers were added or upgraded).   

这些问题同样也会在我们支持的操作系统（linux）上发现，比如安装驱动或更新驱动。

Description: Unsupported Linux operating system and associated drivers, when placed in a new 12G Intel reference architecture, can lead to the operating system identifying errors that have nothing to do with faulty hardware. The same vulnerability was not necessarily the case for 11G servers.  While an unsupported OS is the likely cause of such errors, any OS (whether supported or unsupported) may have such errors that are genuinely the result of faulty hardware. The resolution below includes steps that are helpful in determining if such errors are caused by the OS (unsupported or supported) in conjunction with the drivers, or caused by hardware, and provides steps to resolve if the OS and associated drivers are determined to be the root cause.

Resolution: In an unsupported OS configuration as loaded by the customer, Dell's level of support is "best effort". This means that our tech support will provide support within reason to ensure that the hardware is functioning normally. In the case of errors being generated due to an unsupported OS or the associated drivers, it is recommended for Dell to provide some simple directions to the customer for resolving that problem and allow the customer to handle the matter.
