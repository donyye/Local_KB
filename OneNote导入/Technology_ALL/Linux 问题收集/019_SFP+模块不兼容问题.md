我也share一下之前遇到的一个问题：

![[Technology_ALL_Linux 问题收集_019_SFP+模块不兼容问题_001.png]]

 

解决方法如下（必须使用第三方兼容模块的话，需要做如下改变）：

ixgbe光纤网卡的驱动在默认情况下不支持第三方兼容光模块，会导致网卡驱动加载失败，表现为，执行lspci \|grep 82599能看到网卡在pci设备中

06:00.0 Ethernet controller: Intel Corporation 82599EB 10-Gigabit SFI/SFP+ Network Connection (rev 01)

06:00.1 Ethernet controller: Intel Corporation 82599EB 10-Gigabit SFI/SFP+ Network Connection (rev 01)

但是使用ifconfig -a命令，却找不到网卡，使用demsg可以看到以下出错信息：

ixgbe 0000:06:00.0: failed to load because an unsupported SFP+ module type was detected

解决问题的方法是，执行rmmod ixgbe，卸载掉驱动，然后执行

modprobe ixgbe allow_unsupported_sfp=1,1 

驱动对allow_unsupported_sfp的解释为：

allow_unsupported_sfp:Allow unsupported and untested SFP+ modules on 82599-based adapters (uint)

重新加载后，大部分情况下ifconfig就能够看到网卡信息了。

For your information,

I have R620/R630 with intel X520 cases happened. It caused the servers can't connect with Huawei/H3C 10G fiber Switch in Linux environment. Customer purchase some X520 from dell and reseller. The reseller provide SFP to customer.

 

Symptom:

 

·         Some PE R620/R630 install X520 Nic  can't connect with core 10G fiber Switch.  

![[Technology_ALL_Linux 问题收集_019_SFP+模块不兼容问题_002.jpg]]

 

·         Nic status in CentOS7.1 X64 display error information and Zero Rx/TX  

![[Technology_ALL_Linux 问题收集_019_SFP+模块不兼容问题_003.png]]

 

![[Technology_ALL_Linux 问题收集_019_SFP+模块不兼容问题_004.png]]

 

Troubleshooting steps:

 

·         Check the Nic driver and reinstall driver from dell/intel website (often-used commands: iftop, ethtool, lspci, modinfo, modprobe, iperf and qperf)

![[Technology_ALL_Linux 问题收集_019_SFP+模块不兼容问题_005.jpg]]

·         Update Nic driver, issue persist

·         Check the Fiber and switch port, issue persist

·         Check the SFP model and type with EU, TSR and ISR. We found the 3rd SFP is Huawei. Non original part: intel SFP

 

Solution:

 

·          Replace Intel SFP. Issue fixed
