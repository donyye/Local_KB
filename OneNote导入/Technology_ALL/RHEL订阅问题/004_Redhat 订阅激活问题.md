1.  Introduction 

 

Customers can order Red Hat OEM subscriptions with a Dell server (POS order) or separately (APOS order). 

For POS orders they receive a Dell Tag. 

For APOS order they must be referred to the Dell digital locker to retrieve a 16-digit key - a subscription activation number. 

 

 1.1 POS orders 

 

If the customer orders a Red Hat subscription with a Dell server, they receive a Dell Tag, that they have to activate on the [Red Hat Customer Portal](https://access.redhat.com/subscriptions/activate/dell) , (see screenshot below). The customer must be logged in with their Red Hat credentials, in order to access this page. 

After the Dell Tag activated the customer will be able to see their Red Hat subscription on their account

2.  Process for resolving activation issues 

 

It happens sometimes that the activation process fails for the customer (for various reasons), which results in not being able to activate a dell Tag or RH subscription activation number to access their Red Hat product on their RH account. 

 

Customers can either [contact Red Hat](https://www.redhat.com/en/contact#tab.locations.1) themselves, but for a better customer experience, if you are getting such requests from your customer, you can directly reach out to Red Hat Customer Service, avoiding a direct contact between the Customer and Red Hat. 

 

This document will show you what you should gather and request from your customer before contacting Red Hat Customer Service, and how Red Hat will help the customer. 

 

 

 2.2. Gathering the correct information 

 

In order to contact Red Hat Customer Service and get a 1-step resolution, you will need the following information:  

-    The list of affected Service Tags 
-    A copy of the Dell Invoice: This contains important information for Red Hat such as invoicing date, product SKU and product description, and the Service tags. 
-   The end customer Red Hat account number and/or Red Hat login. This information is really important as Red Hat will activate the product directly on the customer's account. 

 

 2.3.Contacting

 

Red Hat Customer Service 

 

 

Send this information to [globalcustomerservice@redhat.com](mailto:globalcustomerservice@redhat.com) Red Hat Customer Service offers a 24/5 email service. Indicating the correct information will allow a rapid response with activation completed upon the first reply. For best results please use the following template: 

 

\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-- 

Dear Red Hat Customer Service, 

 

Please proceed the replacement of these service tags, as per the 

information provided: 

- Customer Name:  
- Customer Red Hat Account number and/or Customer Login: 
- Dell Service tags affected: 

 

Please find attached the pdf invoice(s) or Dell packing slip for the relevant 

machines. 

 

Thanks, 

\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-- 

 

 

 

 3.3. Confirming the resolution to a customer 

 

 

Red Hat Customer Service should get back to you within 2 business days. If you have provided all necessary information, the product will be activated when the confirmation email is being sent out to you. So you can inform your customer that the product is now available in the Red Hat Customer 

Portal. 

 

Important note: 

 

If the Dell Pro support is for 1 year and the purchased subscription is for 1 year  

   1) Dell tag replacement request is sent within the Dell Pro support duration period 

      ➢ the activation subscription start date will match the Dell order shipment date  

   2) Dell Tag replacement request is sent after the Dell Pro support expiration date 

      ➢ the activation subscription start date will match the Dell care/customer's query handling date

 

If the Dell Pro support is for 3 years and the purchased subscription is for 1 year 

 

    1) Dell Tag replacement request is sent within the Pro support duration period and before the subscription expiration date 

       ➢ provide the activation subscription start date that matches the Dell order shipment date 

    2) Dell Tag replacement request is sent within the Pro support duration period but after subscription expiration date 

       ➢ provide the activation subscription start date that matches the query date.
