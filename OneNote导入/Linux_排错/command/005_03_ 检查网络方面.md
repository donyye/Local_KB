3. LLDP

查看系统是否可以关闭lldp

\# cat sos_commands/networking/ethtool\_-i_ens3f0np0

driver: bnxt_en

version: 5.14.0-427.26.1.el9_4.x86_64

firmware-version: 212.0.162.0/pkg 21.60.22.11

expansion-rom-version:

bus-info: 0000:98:00.0

supports-statistics: yes

supports-test: yes

supports-eeprom-access: yes

supports-register-dump: yes

supports-priv-flags: no[    \<\-\-- ]说明不支持

\#如果支持情况下显示是 "supports-priv-flags: yes"

修改方法： ethtool \--set-priv-flags \<interface\> disable-fw-lldp on

命令查看： ethtool \--show-priv-flags \<interface\> \|grep disable-fw-lldp
