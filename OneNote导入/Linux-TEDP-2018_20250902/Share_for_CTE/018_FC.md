\# find /sys/class/fc_host/\*/ -name \'port_name\'           \>\>\>\>\> retrieve path to wwpn entry

 

\# find /sys/class/fc_host/\*/ -name \'node_name         \>\>\>\>\> retrieve path to wwnn entry
