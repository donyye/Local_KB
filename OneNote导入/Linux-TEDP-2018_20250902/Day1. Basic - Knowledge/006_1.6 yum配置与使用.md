1.6 yum配置与使用

本地yum源配置：

 

\[root@localhost \~\]# mount /dev/cdrom /mnt/iso/

mount: block device /dev/sr0 is write-protected, mounting read-only

 

 

\[root@localhost \~\]# vim /etc/yum.repos.d/local.repo

 

\[RHEL69\]

name=RHEL69

baseurl=file:///mnt/iso

enabled=1

gpgcheck=0
