PFA (Proactive Field Advisory) is a Dell campaign / process for low-risk issues, using auto-mail (blast emails) for customer notification. SAM engagement is optional but is allowing a review of the customer list before going live.

PFA（主动现场咨询）是戴尔针对低风险问题的活动/流程，使用自动邮件（群发邮件）通知客户。SAM 参与是可选的，但允许在上线前审查客户名单。

\
PFN/PFR (Proactive Field Notification/Replacement), another Dell campaign / process, operates without creating a service request (SR). Immediate engagement is crucial for mid-high severity issues, tracked through the updating tool and dashboard. SAM reviews the customer list before each process goes live.

PFN/PFR（主动现场通知/更换）是另一个戴尔活动/流程，无需创建服务请求 (SR) 即可运行。立即参与对于中高严重性问题至关重要，可通过更新工具和仪表板进行跟踪。SAM 在每个流程上线前审查客户名单。

 

PFE (Proactive Field Enhancement), a Legacy EMC process, is SR-based, providing remote or onsite assistance. SAM is accountable until a solution is implemented. SAM will be notified when SR is created. (Grouped weekly)

PFE（主动现场增强）是传统 EMC 流程，基于 SR，提供远程或现场协助。在实施解决方案之前，SAM 负责。创建 SR 时将通知 SAM。 （每周分组）

 

All PFx campaigns are managed by the Global Account Field Action (GCFA) team, involving collaboration with Product Groups (PG), intake, and budgeting. SAM engages during campaign execution.

所有 PFx 活动均由全球客户现场行动 (GCFA) 团队管理，涉及与产品组 (PG) 的协作、接收和预算。SAM 在活动执行期间参与。
