我的回复：

This belongs to system tuning, it has a process, there is no one value that fits all production applications, only is tried and observed.

 

The size of max_sectors_kb defaults to the smaller of max_hw_sectors_kb or max_dev_sectors.

﻿

To adjust to the optimal value, recommendations from the storage side are needed, or you can try to find the suitable value yourself.

﻿

Setting a too small max_sectors_kb may lead to more I/O requests, thereby increasing CPU overhead and system load. Therefore, after setting a parameter, it is necessary to continuously observe whether these values bring excessive overhead.
