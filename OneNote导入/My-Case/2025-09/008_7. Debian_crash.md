The customer OS has crash issue.

Solution:

Analyzing the system log, the system was stuck at 06:25 on September 11, and the system was restarted manually until 14:22 on September 12.

From the record analysis, this problem is likely to be related to the i40e driver, it transmit queue time out, the cpu can not deal with, resulting in process congestion triggered the watchdog, the follow-up has not been recovered, resulting in the final system stuck.\
It is recommended to upgrade i40e driver and upgrade FW.
