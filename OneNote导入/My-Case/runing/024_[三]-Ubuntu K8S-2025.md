apiVersion: apps/v1 kind: Deployment metadata:

selector: matchLabels:

app: nginxweb1 replicas: 2 template:

metadata: labels:

app: nginxweb1 spec:

containers:

- name: nginxwebc image: nginx:latest

imagePullPolicy: IfNotPresent ports:

- containerPort: 80

---

apiVersion: v1 kind: Service metadata:

name: nginxweb-service spec:

externalTrafficPolicy: Cluster selector:

app: nginxweb1 ports:

- protocol: TCP

port: 80

targetPort: 80

六、回退k8s集群/重置k8s集群

  --- ---

  --- ---

k8s安装步骤确实繁琐，可能我们某一步错了，或者遇到各种问题，都得重头再来太麻烦。这时我们可以使用kubeadm提供的reset命令来回退集群。

所有节点执行reset命令

kubeadm reset

master节点执行如下命令

rm -rf /root/.kube rm -rf /etc/cni/net.d rm -rf /etc/kubernetes/*

worker节点执行如下命令

rm -rf /root/.kube rm -rf /etc/cni/net.d rm -rf /etc/kubernetes/*

重启docker或containerd服务（你安装的什么运行时就重启什么运行时）

systemctl restart docker

按照4.5～4.7章节重新执行kubeadm init即可
