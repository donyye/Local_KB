There were no further issues, and the case was closed.\

Results after testing. When installing SUSE 12 SP3 on the R750, it did not have the H755 driver, and currently there are no drivers suitable for SUSE 12 H755 on the Dell official website. Installing other versions of the driver failed, and the PERC card could not be driven.

However, when installing SUSE 15 SP5, it could recognize the H755, indicating that it comes with its own driver and does not require manual installation.

The R750 is not compatible with the SUSE12.\
--------------------------------\
Test the driver installation in the customer environment.\
--------------------------------\
Analyzing:

Customers are unable to enter the system normally after upgrading the kernel version.

Solution:\
Based on the information currently available, this issue is suspected to be caused by the new kernel not having the corresponding PERC card driver.

Other key information:

1. R750 is not compatible with SUSE 12 SP3, and this version has also reached its end-of-life (EOL). An upgrade to SUSE 15 is required.

2. The kernel version the customer needs to upgrade to, 4.12.14-95.128, is still SUSE 12 SP4, which is also an incompatible and EOL version.

3. Since this version is EOL and no longer supported, if wish to continue receiving support for this version, need to purchase SUSE's LTSS service separately.

Next step:

Waiting for update.
